About me

I came to computer engineering by a detour. After two years of studying accounting, an extracurricular programming course showed me what I actually loved, and I switched despite the risks and the pressure around me. It is still a decision I am happy with.

What keeps me going is a feeling I first had on a course project far beyond my skills. I taught myself what I was missing, worked through the bugs and the testing, and when it finally ran I was not thinking about the grade, only: “I actually did it.” I have looked for challenges like that ever since.

Along the way, projects drew me toward artificial intelligence and IoT, and then toward what they can do for medicine. My thesis, an IoT fire and smoke detection system with a real-time dashboard, is one step on that path. I finished ranked 1st among the graduates of my semester, with a GPA of 18.91/20, and I now hope to join a master’s programme in AI and health technology, where teamwork and research can take me further.
